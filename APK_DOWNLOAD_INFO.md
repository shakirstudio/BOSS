## ⬇️ How to Download & Install

### Method 1: Download from the Code Section
1. In the file explorer on the left, locate **`BOSS-app-debug.apk`**.
2. Right-click on **`BOSS-app-debug.apk`** and select **Download** (or click the download icon next to the file name).
3. Transfer or open the `.apk` file on your Android smartphone or tablet.

### Method 2: Download via the Website (One-Tap)
1. Open the website preview (`website/index.html` or the shared web app URL).
2. Tap the floating **"Get APK (24MB)"** button at the bottom of the screen or the **"Download BOSS-app-debug.apk"** button in the hero section.
3. The APK will immediately download to your device.

### Method 3: Export via AI Studio Settings Menu
- Click the **Settings / Export** menu in the top bar of AI Studio.
- Select **Export as APK / AAB** or **Export as ZIP** to download the complete build and binary.

---

## 📱 Installation on Android Device (30 Seconds)
1. Open your Android device's **Downloads** folder or notification shade.
2. Tap **`BOSS-app-debug.apk`**.
3. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings** and toggle **"Allow from this source"**.
4. Tap **Install** &rarr; **Open**.
5. The BOSS app will launch immediately with full offline SQLite storage, role logins (Super Admin, Photographer, Video Editor, Album Specialist), 18% GST quotations, shoot checklists, and Canvera album tracking!
