Frequently Asked Questions

Does BOSS require an active internet connection to work?

No! BOSS is built with modern Room SQLite local persistence on Android. You can create bookings, mark gear checklists on remote destination weddings with zero connectivity, update editing statuses, and compute GST quotations offline.

How does the individual role-based staff visibility work?

In the Admin Console, studio owners can assign specific staff members (Photographers, Editors, Cinematographers, Album Specialists). When that team member logs in, they only see tasks, shoots, and photo/video projects where they are assigned. Revenue numbers, sensitive client quotes, and other team members' workloads remain locked.

Can I export P&L and GST records for my chartered accountant?

Yes! BOSS features a dedicated Financial Year module that organizes all studio revenue, payments, GST liabilities, and pending client balances. You can export complete CSV statements with one tap directly into Excel or Tally.
